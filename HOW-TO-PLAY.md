# Carnival Memory Match — How to Play

Same URL on phone, tablet, classroom PC, and laptop.

1. Pick a pack (Animals or Numbers).
2. Pick a help language, or leave English only.
3. Type a name. Tap **PLAY** or press **Enter**.
4. Each round has 10 toys in two rows of five.
5. Shoot two toys. If they match, they stay down. If not, they come back.
6. Finish both heats, then all 6 rounds.

**Phone / tablet:** tap a toy.

**Computer / classroom PC:** keys **1–5** are the top row, **6–9** and **0** are the bottom row. **Enter** starts. **Enter** or **Space** plays again after a prize.

Round 1 picture + sound. Round 2 picture + word. Later rounds mix sound, English word, and your language.

Sound is baked MP3 files in `audio/`. There is no cloud TTS.

More help: open `HOW-TO-PLAY.md` on the site, or read the how-to line on the start ticket.
